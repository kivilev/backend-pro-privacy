Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-27

"Backend PRO Analytics" (the "App") is a private tool operated by Denis Kivilev ("we", "the operator") for managing the operator's own Instagram professional account (@pro_backend) and Threads account. The App is not offered to the public and has no sign-up.

1. What data the App processes

  • Data of the operator's own Instagram professional account: profile information, posts, stories, reels, and their statistics (reach, views, likes, saves, shares).
  • Comments left under the operator's posts, including the comment text, the public username of the commenter, and the time of the comment. These are public comments that users chose to publish on Instagram.

The App does not collect passwords, payment data, private messages, or contact lists, and does not access the accounts of other users.

2. Why the data is processed

  • To measure the performance of the operator's own content.
  • To read questions and feedback in comments and to prepare replies, which the operator reviews before anything is posted.

3. Sharing and storage

Data is stored in files on the operator's personal computer and is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or profiling. Data is not transferred to any other service, except that comment text may be processed by an AI assistant used by the operator to draft replies. Public comments are kept for as long as they are useful for answering questions and analysing content, and are deleted on request.

4. Your rights and data deletion

If you left a comment and want data about you removed from the App's records, write to kivilev.d@gmail.com with your Instagram username. We will delete the matching records within 30 days and confirm by email. You can also delete your own comment on Instagram at any time.

5. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly process their data.

6. Changes

We may update this policy; the date at the top shows the latest version.

7. Contact

Denis Kivilev, kivilev.d@gmail.com
